Moroccan black soap - savon beldi - doesn't look like any other soap. It's soft, dark, almost like a paste, and it's the first thing that touches your skin in a traditional hammam. We make it in our Marrakech workshop, so this is not a theory article: it's how the product goes from raw ingredients to the jar, and what we've learned about doing it well.

## What Moroccan Black Soap Actually Is

Black soap is a **potassium soap made from olive oil and olive paste**. That's the key difference from a normal bar of soap:

- **Bar soaps** are usually made with sodium hydroxide, which gives a hard soap.
- **Black soap** is made with potassium hydroxide (potash), which gives a soft, gel-like soap.

Its INCI name usually starts with **Potassium Olivate**, followed by water and, for scented versions, the added oils or extracts. The dark colour comes from the olives themselves, not from charcoal or dye.

## The Ingredients

- **Olive oil and olive paste.** Traditionally from dark, ripe olives. They give the soap its colour and its rich texture.
- **Potassium hydroxide solution.** This is what turns oil into soap (saponification). It's handled with care, because it is caustic before the reaction is complete.
- **Water.**
- **Optional additions** for scented versions: eucalyptus, argan oil, rose, or nila.

## Step by Step: How We Make It

### 1. Preparing the oils
The olive oil and olive paste are weighed and mixed. Consistent raw materials are the first condition for a consistent soap from one batch to the next.

### 2. Saponification
The potassium hydroxide solution is added and the mixture is heated and stirred. This is the slow part: the oil and the potash react and the paste thickens into soap. Rushing this step is the most common cause of poor black soap.

### 3. Resting
The soap is left to rest so the reaction can finish and the texture can settle. A well-rested soap is smooth and even; a rushed one can feel greasy or sting the skin.

### 4. Checking the soap
Before packing, we check the texture, colour and smell of each batch, and that it rinses cleanly. Like all true soaps, black soap is alkaline - that's normal - but a well-made batch should not leave the skin burning or tight.

### 5. Adding scents (optional)
For eucalyptus, argan, rose or nila versions, the additions are mixed in at the end, so they're spread evenly.

### 6. Packing
The soap is filled into tubs for professional use or jars for retail, sealed and labelled with the batch number.

## What Makes Good Black Soap - and What to Avoid

| Good sign | Warning sign |
|---|---|
| Smooth, even, gel-like texture | Lumps, grains, or a layer of oil on top |
| Natural olive smell (or a clear added scent) | Rancid or harsh chemical smell |
| Rinses cleanly | Leaves a greasy film or stings |
| Same colour and texture from batch to batch | Each order looks different |
| Clear ingredient list | "Black soap" with no ingredient list at all |

**A common mistake:** confusing Moroccan black soap with African black soap. African black soap comes from West Africa and is made with plant ashes and other oils such as shea. It's a different product with a different texture and use.

## How It's Used

In the hammam, black soap is spread on warm, damp skin and left for a few minutes. It softens the top layer of skin so the kessa glove can exfoliate it. After rinsing, people usually continue with ghassoul clay, rose water and argan oil.

## Buying Black Soap in Bulk

If you're a spa, a distributor or a brand, ask your supplier:

1. **Who makes it?** The producer or a reseller?
2. **What's in it?** A full ingredient list for each variant.
3. **How is it packed?** Tub sizes for professional use, jar sizes for retail.
4. **How consistent is it?** Ask for two samples from different batches and compare them.
5. **How should it be stored?** Closed container, away from heat and humidity.

## FAQ

### Is Moroccan black soap really black?
It's dark brown to almost black, depending on the olives used. The colour comes from the olives, not from added dye.

### Why is black soap soft?
Because it's made with potassium hydroxide instead of sodium hydroxide. Potassium soaps are naturally soft.

### Can black soap be used on the face?
Many people use it on the body only. If used on the face, apply a small amount, rinse well and avoid the eyes. Patch-test first.

### How long does black soap keep?
Stored closed, away from heat and humidity, it keeps for a long time. Check the shelf life on the product label.
